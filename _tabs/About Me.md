I am currently a Visiting Assistant Professor at <a href="https://www.fgcu.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Florida Gulf Coast University (FGCU)</a>. Prior to joining FGCU, I served as a Graduate Research Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Florida International University (FIU), where I conducted research in the <a href="https://adwise.lab.vcu.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Advanced Wireless and Security (ADWISE)</a> Lab under the supervision of Dr. <a href="https://egr.vcu.edu/directory/kemal.akkaya/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Kemal Akkaya</a>. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering at FIU in 2026. I also earned my Master of Science in Computer Engineering from FIU in 2025 and my Bachelor of Science in 2020, magna cum laude, with a minor in Mathematical Sciences.

My research primarily focuses on cybersecurity, with an emphasis on Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), embedded systems, and critical infrastructure security. I am particularly interested in developing practical security mechanisms for resource-constrained and cyber-physical environments. My broader research interests include Internet of Things (IoT) security, Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS), smart grid security, 5G and next-generation networks, secure wireless communications, and emerging communication technologies.

In addition to research, I am actively involved in teaching and mentoring students. At FGCU, I teach Computer Security and Digital Systems and Architecture, emphasizing the connection among fundamental concepts, hands-on experimentation, and real-world applications.
